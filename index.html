Meet Miranda Scarola

I'm Miranda. I support therapists and helping professionals with the operations of their practice, so they can create space for the work they're ready to grow into.

I work with established clinicians who are ready to expand. The group you keep meaning to launch. The workshop sitting in drafts. The book that won't leave you alone. The body of work that belongs beyond the therapy room.

Most of my clients aren't naturally tech-forward. I translate the tech into plain language for non-tech humans. If you've ever wanted to throw your laptop, I get it. I take the pesky, time-draining tasks off your plate, and keep things moving when the bigger work feels messy or discouraging.

I'm also a steady cheerleader. When you want to give up on the project that matters most, I'm the person in your corner reminding you why you started, helping you hold the vision, and keep going.
